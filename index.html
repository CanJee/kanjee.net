Hi, I'm Hasan. I'm a Senior Software Engineer at Flipp with over 8 years of experience building backend systems.

I own the service behind Flipp's highest-revenue ad product. It handles about 15,000 requests per second, and each one is a real-time pricing decision with money attached, so it has to be fast, correct, and reliable. A lot of my best work started as something I proposed rather than something I was assigned.

Outside of work I volunteer as a Transportation Captain at the National Bank Open, and I built the apps our team uses to schedule and dispatch its drivers. The rest of the time I'm usually outdoors: skiing, scuba diving, climbing rock and ice, hiking, riding, or on a tennis court.

Things I've Built

Two products I built from scratch and run on my own. Both are in real use at the National Bank Open.

shiftcrew.app ↗

ShiftCrew

Shift scheduling for events and volunteer teams

Replaces the Google Form, spreadsheet, and email chain. Volunteers share their availability through one link, with no account or app, and the scheduler builds a fair schedule around everyone's hours. Tennis Canada uses it to schedule about 150 volunteer drivers for the National Bank Open.

  • Automatic scheduler that respects each person's hours, avoids conflicts, and flags any shift it can't fill
  • Custom intake forms, schedule publishing, shift reminders, and newsletters by email
  • Volunteer personal info is encrypted at rest

ridecrew.org ↗

RideCrew

Live ride dispatch for event transportation crews

A live dispatch system for the same transportation team, with a real-time board for dispatchers, a driver app, and text message alerts. Most of the drivers are over 60, so everything is designed to need no training.

  • Real-time dispatch board with drag-and-drop ride assignment
  • React Native driver app with live location and push notifications
  • Sign in with a texted code, so there are no passwords to remember
